Text can tell you a wheeze is "high-pitched and musical" and a crackle is "discrete and popping" — but that distinction only really locks in once you've actually heard both. Listen to real recorded breath sounds below, then test your ear in quiz mode.

A note on these recordings: the wheeze, crackles, and stridor clips are real auscultation recordings sourced from Wikimedia Commons (each individually credited and licensed CC BY-SA). Rhonchi and normal breath sounds aren't hosted here yet — instead, each links out to the Hawaii COPD Coalition's own lung sounds page so you can hear them directly from the source. Coverage is currently limited to the sound types where a properly licensed or directly-linked recording is available — more will be added over time.

Listen & Compare

Click any card to hear the actual sound. Click again to stop it. Play them back to back — that's the fastest way to train your ear.

Quiz Mode

A clip plays, you guess what it is, you find out immediately whether you were right — and why.
