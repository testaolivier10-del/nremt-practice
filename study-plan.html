Suggested path
01 Learn the frameworks firstStart with the key mnemonics — SAMPLE, OPQRST, AVPU, and the rest. These show up constantly in the practice questions, so having them solid first makes everything after this faster.
02 Drill by domainUse the domain-specific mini quizzes to work through each of the six domains individually. This is where you actually find your weak spots, before committing to a full timed exam.
03 Take a full timed examOnce you've drilled each domain at least once, take the full 100-question timed exam to practice pacing and stamina under real exam-like conditions.
04 Clear your missed-question queueAfter each attempt, work through the missed-question review until it's empty. This is the highest-leverage single habit on this site — it directly targets what you actually got wrong.
05 Review the psychomotor side separatelyEverything above covers the cognitive exam. Don't forget the psychomotor skills study guide for the hands-on skills week — it's a genuinely separate exam with its own prep.
