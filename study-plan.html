Suggested path

1

Learn the frameworks first

Start with the key mnemonics — SAMPLE, OPQRST, AVPU, and the rest. These show up constantly in the practice questions, so having them solid first makes everything after this faster.

2

Drill by domain

Use the domain-specific mini quizzes to work through each of the six domains individually. This is where you actually find your weak spots, before committing to a full timed exam.

3

Take a full timed exam

Once you've drilled each domain at least once, take the full 100-question timed exam to practice pacing and stamina under real exam-like conditions.

4

Clear your missed-question queue

After each attempt, work through the missed-question review until it's empty. This is the highest-leverage single habit on this site — it directly targets what you actually got wrong.

5

Review the psychomotor side separately

Everything above covers the cognitive exam. Don't forget the psychomotor skills study guide for the hands-on skills week — it's a genuinely separate exam with its own prep.
