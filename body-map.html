Burn surface area — Rule of Nines

The Rule of Nines is a quick field method for estimating total body surface area (TBSA) affected by a burn in an adult, dividing the body into regions that are each roughly 9% (or a multiple of 9%): head & neck 9%, each arm 9%, the entire front torso 18%, the entire back torso 18%, each leg 18%, and the groin 1%.

It's a rough estimate to guide severity communication and transport decisions — not a substitute for burn center evaluation, and pediatric proportions differ (a child's head accounts for a larger share of TBSA).
