Multiple choice tests recall. This tests sequencing and judgment — closer to how the real exam's scenario logic actually works. Every choice here leads somewhere real, not just a right/wrong buzzer. Choose carefully; a delay or a skipped step shows up later in the scenario, exactly like it would on an actual call.
